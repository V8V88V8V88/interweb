02

Add the widget

A small pill that links to the previous and next sites. It picks up your page's text colour and font, so it fits light and dark sites on its own.

light
dark

Paste this where you want it to show up:

<!-- interweb widget --> <script src="https://v8v88v8v88.com/interweb/webring/widget.js" data-ring="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/v8v88v8v88/interweb@main/webring/sites.json" async ></script>

To force one look, add data-theme="light" or data-theme="dark". data-label changes the ring name shown.

03

Custom links

Want your own style? Add three links anywhere on your page and minimal.js fills in where they go.

<!-- your markup --> <a data-interweb="prev" href="#">←</a> <a data-interweb="random" href="#">random</a> <a data-interweb="next" href="#">→</a> <script src="https://v8v88v8v88.com/interweb/webring/minimal.js" data-ring="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/v8v88v8v88/interweb@main/webring/sites.json" async ></script>
04

Join the ring

  1. Add the widget or custom links to your site.
  2. Fill out the join form (needs a GitHub account).
  3. A bot checks the links work on your site. Once your request is approved, you're in the ring.

Open the join form ↗

Prefer a pull request? Add your site to webring/sites.json and open one.

Membership is curated. Not every request is merged.
